How to Measure

1 Height

Measure from the top of the head to the ground. The child should stand without shoes, feet together, and back against a wall.

2 Chest

Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape measure horizontal.

3 Waist

Measure around the natural waistline, usually the narrowest part of the torso.

4 Hips

Measure around the fullest part of the hips and bottom.
