Our Heritage

Weaving Stories Since 2010

What started as a small atelier in Paris has grown into a globally beloved brand, dedicated to preserving the innocence and magic of childhood through exquisite design.

Meet The Founder

Elise Laurent

"When my first daughter was born, I searched everywhere for clothing that married exceptional quality with timeless design, without the use of harsh synthetic dyes or stiff fabrics. Finding none, I decided to create them."

Today, Elise oversees every collection, ensuring that her initial vision remains untouched. Every piece must pass the 'Elise Test': Is it soft? Is it beautiful? Will it last?

Ethical Sourcing

We trace every thread. Our materials are sourced exclusively from farms and mills that uphold the highest standards of environmental and social responsibility.

Master Craftsmanship

Our artisans spend hours perfecting every seam, buttonhole, and hem, ensuring each garment is robust enough for play and elegant enough for parties.

Generational Quality

We don't design for a single season. Our clothing is crafted to be cherished, outgrown, and passed down to younger siblings and friends.
